How It Works: From Oven to Table

Step 1

The Lead Time

I require a 1–2 day lead time after your order is submitted, as everything is made completely fresh for you.

Step 2

Pickup & Local Delivery

I offer local doorstep delivery within a 10 mile radius and contactless porch pickup right here in Herndon. Just choose your preferred day when you order.

Step 3

Cozy Packaging

All of the artisan loaves and sweet treats are carefully packaged in bakery bags and boxes to lock in freshness and warmth.

Step 4

Payment

Once your order is confirmed in the baking schedule, I'll email you a confirmation with your total and a Venmo link. Prefer cash? No problem, you can pay at pickup or delivery.
